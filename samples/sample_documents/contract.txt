MASTER SERVICE AGREEMENT

This Master Service Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of January 15, 2024, by and between
Acme Corporation, a Delaware corporation ("Client"), and DataTech Solutions, Inc., a California
corporation ("Provider").

1. SERVICES

Provider agrees to provide data migration and analytics consulting services as specified in the
Statement of Work attached as Exhibit A ("Services").

2. TERM

This Agreement shall commence on January 15, 2024 and continue for one (1) year unless earlier
terminated by either party in accordance with Section 10 of this Agreement.

3. FEES AND PAYMENT

Client shall pay Provider the fees as outlined in Exhibit A. Invoices shall be submitted monthly
and payment shall be due within thirty (30) days of invoice date.

4. CONFIDENTIALITY

Each party agrees to maintain the confidentiality of all proprietary information disclosed by the
other party, including but not limited to technical data, business plans, and customer lists.

5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY RIGHTS

All work product, including source code, documentation, and analysis reports, shall be the exclusive
property of Client upon full payment of all fees.

6. WARRANTIES

Provider warrants that it has the right to enter into this Agreement and that the Services will be
performed in a professional and workmanlike manner consistent with industry standards.

7. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

Neither party shall be liable for indirect, incidental, consequential, or punitive damages arising
out of or related to this Agreement or the Services.

8. INSURANCE

Provider shall maintain professional liability insurance with minimum coverage of $1,000,000 per
occurrence and $2,000,000 aggregate throughout the term of this Agreement.

9. COMPLIANCE

Provider shall comply with all applicable laws and regulations in performing the Services, including
data protection and privacy laws such as GDPR, CCPA, and HIPAA where applicable.

10. TERMINATION

Either party may terminate this Agreement with thirty (30) days written notice if the other party
materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure such breach within fifteen (15) days of
receiving written notice of the breach.

11. GOVERNING LAW

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of
California, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.
